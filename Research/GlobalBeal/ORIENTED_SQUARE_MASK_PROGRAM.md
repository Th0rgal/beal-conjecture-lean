# New direction: an orientation-sensitive mask hierarchy

The mask itself is not enough; its position relative to the equality matters.

## Type I: two square masks on the positive summands

\[
X^{2p}+Y^{2q}=Z^r.
\]

For 29 explicit right primes this sector is effectively finite. The next task
is computational: determine \(V_r\), enumerate the low prime pairs, and run
finite Hilbert-newform elimination.

## Type II: one square mask on a summand and one on the right

\[
X^{2p}+Y^q=Z^{2r}.
\]

This is not a plus equation after reorientation. It instead admits the exact
coupled factorization

\[
v^q-u^q=2X^p,\qquad
v^q+u^q=2Z^r.
\]

The right tool is a synchronized cyclotomic multi-Frey method.

## Type III: one square mask

\[
X^{2p}+Y^q=Z^r.
\]

This requires a genuinely asymmetric compatible system. The first finite
boundary is the square-masked parent of

\[
A^4+B^5=C^7.
\]

2. Build Frey representations for the two pure cyclotomic equations in the

3. Recompute the \((2,5,7)\) HGM conductor after imposing the one-square mask.
