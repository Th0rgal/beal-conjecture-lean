## Corrections incorporated

### Absolute irreducibility is not an `F_125^*` order argument

The observation `3,7` do not divide `|F_125^*|=124` does not prove absolute
irreducibility over an algebraic closure. The valid replacement is the
prime-to-`5` dihedral criterion: for a local type
`Ind_{W_E}^{W_F}(kappa)`, reduction preserves irreducibility when the order of
`kappa/kappa^sigma` is prime to `5`. The order-`4`, order-`12`, and order-`28`
types used here satisfy that condition.

### The four-prime `Q` sieve treated only the trivial ray character

A reducible fixed-`7` representation may contain any of eight ray characters in
`Cl_{3*(sqrt(5))*infinity_1*infinity_2} ~= C4 x C2`. The new checker evaluates
all eight. Prime `17` removes the order-`4` characters; the multiplicative
signs at `19` and `11` isolate `eta=(2,0)`; primes `71` and `13` then impose the
support restrictions above.

### The old low-level modular pair is vacuous

The earlier low-level bridge paired the CM packet
`3.3.49.1-729.1-b` with fixed-`7` packets `24,28`. The global mod-`5` non-CM
certificate now excludes every CM specialization for a primitive solution, so
that packet cannot occur. The fixed-`7` compression to `24,28` remains valid;
the claimed pair with `729.1-b` does not remain in the frontier.

### Even-branch conductor exponent `e7=1` was a variable-orientation error

For the mod-`5` HGM use

```text
(a,b,c)=(-C,B,A), (q,p,r)=(7,5,3).
```

Dahmen--Siksek's even branch has `7` not dividing `A*B`, hence `q` does not
divide `b*c`. Table 3.5 therefore gives exponent `2` in the reducible
polynomial case and exponent `3` in the irreducible polynomial case. Packet
`3.3.49.1-189.1-a`, whose exponent pair is `(1,1)`, is not an even-branch
candidate. The remaining packet `1323.1-a` is eliminated by the already
recorded order-`5` inertia mismatch.
