# Audit of the 2025 “strong Fermat–Catalan” claim

## Result

The final decomposition-group implication in arXiv:2509.18275v1 is not valid.
The paper may still state a true theorem, but its published argument does not
establish the claimed complete splitting and therefore cannot currently be used
to close all repeated-prime Beal signatures.

The finite group calculation and the corrected order sieve are replayed by:

```bash
python3 scripts/check_strong_fermat_catalan_audit.py --self-test
python3 scripts/check_strong_fermat_catalan_audit.py
```

## 1. The step under audit

Put

```text
K  = Q(zeta_p),
K' = Q(zeta_q),
L  = K K'.
```

Since distinct prime cyclotomic fields are linearly disjoint,

```text
Gal(L/Q) = G x H,
G = Gal(K/Q),
H = Gal(K'/Q).
```

In Lemma 6, the preprint obtains, for a rational prime `r` and a prime of `K`
above it, complete splitting in `L/K`. In decomposition-group notation this is
recorded as

```text
D_r ∩ H = {1}.
```

The paper then states that the fixed field of `D_r` contains `K'`, and hence
that the rational prime `r` splits completely in `K'/Q`.

The implication is false. The inclusion

```text
K' = L^G  subset  L^D
```

is equivalent to

```text
D subset G,
```

not to `D ∩ H = {1}`.

## 2. Minimal group counterexample

Take

```text
G = C2,
H = C2,
D = <(1,1)> subset G x H.
```

Then

```text
D ∩ H = {1},
```

but the projection of `D` to `H` is nontrivial. Thus `D` is not contained in
`G`, and `L^D` need not contain `K'`.

This is not an artificial pathology: both cyclotomic Galois groups have an
order-two complex-conjugation quotient, so diagonal decomposition subgroups are
compatible with the actual group orders in the paper.

## 3. Exact Frobenius calculation

Let `ell` be unramified in both cyclotomic fields and put

```text
m = ord_p(ell),
n = ord_q(ell).
```

The rational decomposition group is cyclic, generated by

```text
(Frob_p(ell), Frob_q(ell))
```

inside `C_m x C_n`.

Its intersection with the second factor is trivial exactly when

```text
n | m.
```

Indeed, a power of the generator lies in the second factor precisely when its
exponent is a multiple of `m`; the resulting second component generates a
subgroup of order `n/gcd(m,n)`.

By contrast, `ell` splits completely in `K'/Q` exactly when

```text
n = 1,
```

or equivalently `ell = 1 mod q`.

Therefore the correct implication is only

```text
ord_q(ell) | ord_p(ell).
```

## 4. Concrete arithmetic counterexample to the implication

Choose

```text
p = 11,
q = 31,
ell = 2.
```

Then

```text
ord_11(2) = 10,
ord_31(2) = 5.
```

Hence the relative splitting criterion holds because `5 | 10`, while `2` is
not congruent to `1 mod 31`; it does not split completely in
`Q(zeta_31)`.

Thus the exact group-theoretic pattern used in the final argument can occur
without the claimed absolute splitting.

## 5. What can be salvaged

Assume, for audit purposes, that every preceding ideal-theoretic step of the
preprint is correct and that the even prime divisor does yield the relative
splitting statement. Since one of `x`, `y`, and `x+y` is even and removing a
power of the odd prime `q` preserves parity, the prime `2` occurs in the
relevant product. The valid conclusion is

```text
ord_q(2) | ord_p(2).
```

Consequently

```text
q | 2^ord_p(2) - 1.
```

This is a useful finite sieve for `q` once `p` is fixed, but it is not a
contradiction. Examples:

```text
p=5  -> q in {3},
p=7  -> no distinct odd-prime q,
p=11 -> q in {3,31},
p=13 -> q in {3,5,7},
p=17 -> q in {3,5}.
```

For instance, the pair `(p,q)=(11,31)` survives the corrected condition.

## 6. Consequence for the Beal program

The preprint must not be registered as a literature proof of

```text
X^p + Y^p = Z^q
```

for all distinct odd primes `p>3` and `q`.

The safe repeated-prime frontier remains:

1. an unconditional Barlow–Abel/cyclotomic perfect-power split;
2. exact prime-order restrictions on nonexceptional factors;
3. the unresolved global cyclotomic or modular elimination;
4. a separate treatment of the repeated exponent `p=3` and of difference
   orientations.

The audit identifies a precise repair target: any valid replacement must rule
out nontrivial *graph* decomposition groups, rather than merely proving trivial
intersection with one direct-product factor.
